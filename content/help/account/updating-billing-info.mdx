<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Viewing your current plan</h2>
<p className="body-text">Your current plan (Studio or Studio Pro) and active add-on modules are visible in your dashboard. The <strong>Add-on Settings</strong> tab shows which modules are enabled, and the sidebar displays your account info.</p>
<p className="body-text">To see your plan's included limits — SMS count, email count, and available features — refer to the <strong>Plans and pricing</strong> article or check your dashboard settings.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Updating your payment method</h2>
<p className="body-text">Your OVIAH subscription is billed monthly. To update the credit card or payment method on file for your subscription, go to <strong>Business Settings</strong> in your dashboard. Your billing is managed securely through Stripe — OVIAH doesn't store your card details directly.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Upgrading or changing plans</h2>
<p className="body-text">To upgrade from Studio to Studio Pro, look for the upgrade option in your dashboard settings. The change takes effect immediately — you'll get instant access to SMS reminders, waitlist, chat, and all Studio Pro features. Your billing adjusts on the next cycle.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">If you have questions about your billing or need to make changes not available in the dashboard, reach out to <strong>support@oviah.io</strong> and we'll handle it directly.</div>
</div>
</div>
