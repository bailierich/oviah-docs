<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Your notifications</h2>
<p className="body-text">As a business owner, you receive notifications for key events in your dashboard and via email. When a client books, cancels, or makes a payment, you get an in-app notification and an email alert. These keep you informed without needing to constantly check your dashboard.</p>
<p className="body-text">The notification bell in your dashboard sidebar shows unread notifications. Click it to see recent activity.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Client notification settings</h2>
<p className="body-text">Each client profile has a <strong>communication preference</strong> field: email, SMS, both, or none. This controls what automated messages they receive. You can also toggle email and SMS notifications individually per client.</p>
<p className="body-text">Respecting these preferences is automatic — OVIAH checks the client's settings before sending any reminder or follow-up.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Automation settings</h2>
<p className="body-text">All automated messaging is configured under <strong>Reminders</strong> in the Business Operations menu. Here's what you can control:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Email reminders</div>
<div className="step-desc">Toggle on/off. Set timing to 24 hours, 2 hours, or both before the appointment. Available on all plans.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">SMS reminders</div>
<div className="step-desc">Toggle on/off. Same timing options as email. Requires Studio Pro or an add-on. Uses your monthly SMS allowance.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Follow-up emails</div>
<div className="step-desc">Sent after an appointment. Choose timing: 24 hours, 48 hours, 1 week, 2 weeks, or 1 month. Great for asking for reviews or prompting rebooking.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Birthday wishes</div>
<div className="step-desc">Automated birthday email to clients with a birthday on file. Studio Pro only.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Books-open notifications</h2>
<p className="body-text">If you use the monthly booking mode (books open on a specific day each month), you can notify subscribers when bookings open. This sends an email blast — and optionally an SMS — to everyone on your subscriber list. Available on Studio Pro.</p>
<p className="body-text">Your total subscriber count and notification stats are visible in the Reminders settings.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Keep your SMS allowance in check by being selective about which reminders use text messages. The 2-hour email + SMS combo is the most effective, but sending every notification via SMS can eat through your monthly limit. Check your usage in the dashboard regularly.</div>
</div>
</div>
