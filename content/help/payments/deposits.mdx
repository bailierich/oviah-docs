<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Why require deposits</h2>
<p className="body-text">Deposits protect your time. When a client puts money down to book, they're far more likely to show up. Even a modest deposit — $20 to $25 — dramatically cuts no-shows and signals that you run a professional operation.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">How to enable deposits</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">1</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Go to Payments settings</div>
<div className="step-desc">Open <strong>Payments</strong> from your dashboard settings. Make sure you've already connected Stripe or Square — deposits require an active payment provider.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">2</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Toggle Require Deposit on</div>
<div className="step-desc">This enables deposit collection for all bookings made through your booking page.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">3</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Choose your deposit type</div>
<div className="step-desc">You have two options: <strong>Fixed amount</strong> (e.g. $25 per booking regardless of service) or <strong>Percentage</strong> (e.g. 25% of the service price). For percentage deposits, you can also set a minimum and maximum to keep things predictable.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">4</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Set when to collect</div>
<div className="step-desc">Choose <strong>At booking</strong> to collect immediately, or enable <strong>Pay Later</strong> to give clients a grace period (default 24 hours) to complete the deposit after they reserve a slot.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Payment links</h2>
<p className="body-text">Need to collect a deposit or balance outside the booking flow? You can send a <strong>payment link</strong> directly from a booking's detail panel. Payment links are unique, secure, and expire after 7 days. Clients click the link, pay via Stripe or Square, and the booking is automatically updated.</p>
<p className="body-text">This is useful for collecting the remaining balance before an appointment or for rescheduled bookings where the original deposit was already applied.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">If you offer high-ticket services (like installs or color corrections), consider using a percentage deposit with a minimum. That way a $300 service collects a meaningful deposit, but a $50 service doesn't ask for an uncomfortably large upfront amount.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">How deposits appear to clients</h2>
<p className="body-text">During booking, clients see the deposit amount clearly before they confirm. After payment, their booking status shows as <strong>Deposit Paid</strong> with the remaining balance displayed. You can track deposit status, balance due, and payment history for every booking in your dashboard.</p>
</div>
