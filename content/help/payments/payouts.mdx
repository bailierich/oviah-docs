<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">How payouts work</h2>
<p className="body-text">When a client pays through your booking page, the money goes directly to your connected Stripe or Square account — OVIAH never holds your funds. Payouts from your payment provider to your bank account happen on a rolling schedule, typically every 2 business days once your account is fully verified.</p>
<p className="body-text">The exact payout timing depends on your payment provider and how long you've had your account. New Stripe accounts may see a 7-day initial hold; after that, payouts arrive on the standard rolling schedule.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Fees</h2>
<p className="body-text">Transaction fees are <strong>2.9% + $0.30</strong> per payment — the standard rate from Stripe and Square. OVIAH does not charge any additional platform fee on top of this. What your provider charges is all you pay.</p>
<p className="body-text">Manual payments (cash, check, Venmo, CashApp) recorded in OVIAH have no transaction fee since they don't go through a payment processor.</p>
</div>
