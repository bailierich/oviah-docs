<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">What is OVIAH Pay?</h2>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH Pay is the built-in payment system that lets you connect Stripe or Square through <strong>Settings &gt; Payments</strong> and start collecting deposits the same day. You connect your own account via a secure OAuth flow — your money goes directly to you and OVIAH never holds your funds.</p>
<p className="body-text">You can also accept manual payment methods like cash, check, Venmo, or CashApp and record them in your dashboard for complete booking records.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Connecting Stripe</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">1</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Open Payments settings</div>
<div className="step-desc">From your OVIAH dashboard, go to <strong>Payments</strong> in the settings menu. You'll see connection cards for each supported provider.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">2</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Click Connect on Stripe</div>
<div className="step-desc">You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure onboarding flow. If you already have a Stripe account, OVIAH will detect it and let you link it directly — no need to create a new one.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">3</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Verify your identity</div>
<div className="step-desc">Stripe will ask for your legal name, date of birth, and last four of your SSN (or full EIN if you're a business). This is required by financial regulations and keeps your account secure.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">4</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Add your bank account</div>
<div className="step-desc">Enter your bank routing and account number for payouts. Stripe typically sends a small test deposit within 1–2 business days to verify the account.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Connecting Square</h2>
<p className="body-text">The process for Square is similar — click <strong>Connect</strong> on the Square card in your Payments settings and authorize OVIAH through Square's OAuth flow. If you already process payments through Square, this lets you keep everything in one place.</p>
<p className="body-text">Square supports credit cards and integrates with your existing Square ecosystem if you use their hardware or point-of-sale system.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">You can connect both Stripe and Square, then choose a preferred provider in your payment settings. OVIAH will use your preferred provider for online transactions but you can always switch.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">After you're connected</h2>
<p className="body-text">Once connected, your dashboard will show the account ID, connection date, and last used date for each provider. You can immediately start requiring deposits on bookings and collecting payments.</p>
<p className="body-text">Transaction fees are 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction — the standard rate charged by Stripe and Square directly. There are no additional OVIAH platform fees on top of that. You can view all transactions, pending payouts, and payout history from the <strong>Reports</strong> tab at any time.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Manual payment methods</h2>
<p className="body-text">Not every client pays online. You can record cash, check, Venmo, and CashApp payments directly in the booking details so your records stay complete. Manual payments won't trigger a digital receipt, but the booking will be marked as paid in your system.</p>
</div>
