<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Schedule blocks</h2>
<p className="body-text">Schedule blocks let you mark time as unavailable so clients can't book during those windows. Use them for vacations, personal appointments, lunch breaks, or anything else that takes you off the clock.</p>
<p className="body-text">You can create blocks from two places: the <strong>Availability & Hours</strong> settings or directly on your <strong>Schedule</strong> tab by clicking <strong>Create Schedule Block</strong>.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Types of blocks</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Single date</div>
<div className="step-desc">Block a specific day — for example, a doctor's appointment on March 15. Choose the date and optionally set start/end times if you're only blocking part of the day.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Date range</div>
<div className="step-desc">Block a stretch of days — for a vacation, a training, or a move. Set a start and end date and OVIAH removes availability for the entire range.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Recurring</div>
<div className="step-desc">Block a repeating window — like a lunch break every day from 12–1pm, or a standing personal commitment every Tuesday afternoon. Set the pattern once and it repeats automatically.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Adding details to blocks</h2>
<p className="body-text">Every block has a <strong>title</strong> (e.g. "Lunch Break," "Vacation," "Personal") and an optional <strong>notes</strong> field for your own reference. These details are visible only to you — clients just see that the time is unavailable.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">If you take a lunch break at the same time every day, set it as a recurring block instead of adjusting your business hours. That way your morning and afternoon availability stays clean and clients can still book on either side of the break.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Business hours vs. blocks</h2>
<p className="body-text">Your <strong>business hours</strong> (set in Availability & Hours) define your regular weekly schedule — which days you work, what time you start and end. Schedule blocks are <em>overrides</em> on top of that. They let you make exceptions without changing your regular hours.</p>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH calculates available time slots in real time by combining your business hours, schedule blocks, existing bookings, and each service's duration plus buffer time. Clients always see accurate availability.</p>
</div>
