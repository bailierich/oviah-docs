<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">How reminders work</h2>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH automatically sends reminders to clients before their appointments. You configure the timing and channels once, and the automation system handles everything — no manual follow-up needed.</p>
<p className="body-text">All reminder settings live under <strong>Reminders</strong> in the Business Operations menu of your dashboard.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Email reminders</h2>
<p className="body-text">Email reminders are available on all plans. You can send them at multiple intervals — the most common setup is <strong>24 hours</strong> and <strong>2 hours</strong> before the appointment. Toggle each timing on or off depending on what works for your clients.</p>
<p className="body-text">Emails include the appointment date, time, service, and your business info. They're sent via Resend so they land in inboxes reliably.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">SMS reminders</h2>
<p className="body-text">SMS reminders are available on <strong>Studio Pro</strong> or as an add-on. Text messages get opened faster than emails and are especially effective for same-day reminders. Like email, you can configure <strong>24-hour</strong> and <strong>2-hour</strong> timing.</p>
<p className="body-text">SMS messages are sent via Twilio. Your plan includes a monthly SMS allowance — 500 on Studio, 2,000 on Studio Pro. Usage beyond that is $0.02 per message.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">The 2-hour SMS reminder is the single most effective no-show prevention tool. Clients who get a text the morning of or a couple hours before are significantly more likely to show up or reschedule in time for you to fill the slot.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Follow-up emails</h2>
<p className="body-text">After an appointment, OVIAH can send an automated follow-up email. Choose a timing that fits your style: <strong>24 hours</strong>, <strong>48 hours</strong>, <strong>1 week</strong>, <strong>2 weeks</strong>, or <strong>1 month</strong> after the visit. Use it to thank clients, ask for reviews, or prompt them to rebook.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Birthday wishes</h2>
<p className="body-text">If you have clients' birthdays on file, OVIAH can send an automated birthday email. It's a small touch that builds loyalty and keeps your name in their inbox at a moment that feels personal. Available on <strong>Studio Pro</strong>.</p>
</div>
