<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">The no-show problem</h2>
<p className="body-text">No-shows cost you time and money. An empty chair that could have been filled by someone else means lost revenue you can't recover. OVIAH gives you multiple tools to prevent no-shows and deal with repeat offenders.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Prevention tools</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">1</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Require deposits</div>
<div className="step-desc">The most effective tool. When clients put money down, they show up. Set a fixed amount or percentage under <strong>Payments</strong> settings. Even $20–$25 makes a significant difference.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">2</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Enable reminders</div>
<div className="step-desc">Turn on email and SMS reminders under <strong>Reminders</strong>. The 2-hour reminder is especially effective — it gives clients enough time to let you know if they can't make it.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">3</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Set cancellation policies</div>
<div className="step-desc">Configure cancellation policies under <strong>Business Settings</strong>. These are displayed to clients during booking so they know the rules upfront. Clear policies reduce disputes and set expectations.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Tracking no-shows</h2>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH automatically tracks no-shows and cancellations per client. In any client's profile you can see their <strong>no-show count</strong>, <strong>cancellation count</strong>, <strong>total bookings</strong>, and <strong>completion rate</strong>. This helps you spot patterns — a client who cancels repeatedly might not be worth the slot.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Blocking clients</h2>
<p className="body-text">If a client is a repeat no-show, you can block them from booking. Go to their profile in the <strong>Clients</strong> tab, click the block option, and add a reason (e.g. "3 no-shows, no communication"). Blocked clients cannot book through your booking page.</p>
<p className="body-text">You can unblock a client at any time if circumstances change. The block reason and timestamp are recorded in their profile for your reference.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Before blocking someone, consider sending a personal message first. Sometimes a quick "Hey, I noticed you've missed a few appointments — is everything okay?" rebuilds the relationship. Reserve blocking for clients who don't respond or show a clear pattern.</div>
</div>
</div>
