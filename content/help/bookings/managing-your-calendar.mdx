<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Calendar views</h2>
<p className="body-text">Your <strong>Schedule</strong> tab is the command center of your day. OVIAH offers three ways to view your appointments:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Week View</div>
<div className="step-desc">A 7-day calendar grid showing all booked slots at a glance. Best for planning your week ahead.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Day View</div>
<div className="step-desc">A detailed single-day view with time slots. Ideal when you're working and need to see what's coming next.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">List View</div>
<div className="step-desc">A tabular list of upcoming appointments. Easy to scan and search through.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
<p className="body-text">Navigate between days or weeks with the arrow buttons, or jump back to today with the <strong>Go to Today</strong> button. Your calendar updates in real time — when a client books online, it appears instantly.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Creating an appointment</h2>
<p className="body-text">Click any open time slot on your calendar to create a new appointment. Select the client, service, add-ons, and time. You can also create appointments from the <strong>Create Booking</strong> button at the top of the Schedule tab.</p>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH uses atomic booking — a database-level lock prevents double-bookings even if two people try to book the same slot at the exact same moment. Duration-aware overlap detection accounts for the full service length plus your buffer time.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Appointment details</h2>
<p className="body-text">Click any appointment to open the details panel. From here you can:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">View or edit the booking</div>
<div className="step-desc">See the client, service, time, payment status, add-ons, notes, and source (direct, waitlist, Instagram, referral, etc.).</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Collect payment</div>
<div className="step-desc">Process a payment directly or send a secure payment link that expires in 7 days.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Reschedule or cancel</div>
<div className="step-desc">Move the appointment to a new time or cancel it with automatic client notification.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Use the search bar at the top of the Schedule tab to quickly find appointments by client name, service, or notes. Saves time when you're looking up a specific booking mid-day.</div>
</div>
</div>
