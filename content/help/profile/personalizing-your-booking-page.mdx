<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">The page builder</h2>
<p className="body-text">Your booking page at <strong>[yourname].oviah.me</strong> is fully customizable through the page builder in your dashboard. You control the look, feel, and layout — no code required. Studio Pro members get access to the custom frontend builder; Studio members use the template-based builder.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">What you can customize</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Theme colors</div>
<div className="step-desc">Set your <strong>primary</strong>, <strong>secondary</strong>, and <strong>text</strong> colors. These are applied across your entire booking page — buttons, headings, accents, and more. OVIAH generates a brand palette from your primary color to keep everything cohesive.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Typography</div>
<div className="step-desc">Choose a <strong>heading font</strong> and <strong>body font</strong>, plus adjust body text size. Fonts carry your personality — a serif font feels different from a clean sans-serif.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Background</div>
<div className="step-desc">Set a solid color, gradient, or background image for your page. This is the first thing clients see when they land on your link.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Section layout</div>
<div className="step-desc">Arrange, show, or hide sections on your page. Control the order of your services, about section, gallery, policies, and more. Each section has its own position and visibility settings.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Gallery page</div>
<div className="step-desc">Showcase your work with photos. Available on all plans.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Policies page</div>
<div className="step-desc">Display your cancellation, no-show, and booking policies. Available on all plans.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Waitlist page</div>

</div>
</div>

</div>
