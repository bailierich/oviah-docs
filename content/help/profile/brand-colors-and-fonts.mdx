<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Setting your colors</h2>
<p className="body-text">In the page builder, you can set three core colors that define your booking page's look:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Primary color</div>
<div className="step-desc">Used for buttons, active states, and key accents. This is the color clients associate with your brand. OVIAH automatically generates a complementary palette from your primary color.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Secondary color</div>
<div className="step-desc">Used for backgrounds, borders, and softer accents. Pairs with your primary to create visual depth.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Text color</div>
<div className="step-desc">Controls body text, labels, and headings. Make sure there's enough contrast with your background for readability.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Choosing fonts</h2>
<p className="body-text">You can select separate fonts for <strong>headings</strong> and <strong>body text</strong>, plus adjust the body text size. Typography sets the tone — a modern sans-serif feels different from an elegant serif. Pick what matches your vibe.</p>
<p className="body-text">Fonts are applied across your entire booking page: service names, descriptions, headers, buttons, and confirmation screens all use your selections.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Background options</h2>
<p className="body-text">The page builder gives you three background options:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Solid color</div>
<div className="step-desc">A clean single-color background. Works well with bold brand colors or neutral tones.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Gradient</div>
<div className="step-desc">A smooth transition between two colors. Adds visual interest without being distracting.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Image</div>
<div className="step-desc">Upload a background image for a fully custom look. Works best with subtle textures or lifestyle photography.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Pull your brand colors directly from your Instagram feed or existing marketing materials. Consistency across touchpoints — your booking page, your social media, your physical space — makes you look polished and intentional.</div>
</div>
</div>
