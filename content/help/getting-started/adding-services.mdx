<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Creating a service</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">1</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Open Services & Pricing</div>
<div className="step-desc">From your dashboard, go to <strong>Services & Pricing</strong> in the Business Operations menu.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">2</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Click Add Service</div>
<div className="step-desc">Enter the service <strong>name</strong>, <strong>description</strong>, <strong>duration</strong> (in minutes — default is 60), and <strong>price</strong>. These are the details clients see when they're choosing what to book.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">3</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Assign a category</div>
<div className="step-desc">Select an existing category or create a new one. Categories help clients browse your services — especially if you offer multiple types (e.g. "Hair," "Nails," "Skincare"). Each category has a name, description, and color label.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">4</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Set the service type</div>
<div className="step-desc">Choose whether this is a <strong>main</strong> service (bookable on its own), an <strong>add-on</strong> (only available with another service), or <strong>both</strong>. This controls how it appears in your booking flow.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Add-on services</h2>
<p className="body-text">Add-ons let clients customize their booking. A deep conditioning treatment, a scalp massage, or extra lash volume — these are services a client can add on top of a main service during booking.</p>
<p className="body-text">When creating an add-on, you can configure:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Compatible services</div>
<div className="step-desc">Choose which main services this add-on can be paired with. For example, a "Deep Condition" add-on might only be compatible with "Wash & Style" and "Color Treatment."</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Additional duration</div>
<div className="step-desc">If the add-on extends the appointment, toggle <strong>Affects Duration</strong> on and set the additional minutes. OVIAH automatically adjusts the calendar to account for the longer booking.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Add-on price</div>
<div className="step-desc">Set a separate price for the add-on. This is added to the main service total. If the add-on is complimentary, set the price to $0.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Add-ons are a great way to increase your average booking value without creating dozens of separate services. A $15 add-on offered at checkout can meaningfully add up over a month of bookings.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Managing categories</h2>
<p className="body-text">Switch to the <strong>Categories</strong> tab in Services & Pricing to create, edit, or delete categories. Each category has a color — this color appears as a label on your booking page, making it easy for clients to scan your offerings at a glance.</p>
<p className="body-text">You can deactivate a service or category at any time without deleting it. Deactivated items won't appear on your booking page but remain in your records.</p>
</div>
