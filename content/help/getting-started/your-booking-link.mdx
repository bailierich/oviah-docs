<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Your link format</h2>
<p className="body-text">Every OVIAH account gets a booking page at <strong>[yourname].oviah.me</strong>. This is your live, public booking page — clients visit it to browse your services, pick a time, and book directly. It's active the moment your account is created.</p>
<p className="body-text">Your booking page is fully branded with your colors, fonts, logo, and business info from the page builder. It works on any device — desktop, tablet, or phone.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Where to share it</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">1</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Instagram bio</div>
<div className="step-desc">Drop your link in your Instagram bio so followers can book in one tap. If you use a link-in-bio tool, add it as a prominent button.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">2</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Text it to clients</div>
<div className="step-desc">Send your link directly to existing clients via text or DM. It's the fastest way to get someone booked — no app download needed.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">3</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Add it to your website</div>
<div className="step-desc">OVIAH also offers an embeddable version of your booking page. Use the <strong>/embed</strong> path to drop it into an iframe on your existing website so clients never leave your site.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">You can link directly to a specific service by adding <strong>?service=ServiceName</strong> to your URL. For example, if you're promoting a blowout special on Instagram, link to <strong>yourname.oviah.me/booking?service=Blowout</strong> so clients land right on that service.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">What clients see</h2>
<p className="body-text">When someone visits your link, they see your branded booking page with all active services organized by category. They choose a service (and any add-ons), pick a date and time from your real-time availability, enter their contact info, and pay a deposit if you require one. The entire flow takes under two minutes.</p>
<p className="body-text">After booking, clients receive a confirmation email with the appointment details and a link to cancel if needed. Reminders are sent automatically based on your automation settings.</p>
</div>
