<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Before you begin</h2>
<p className="body-text">Getting set up takes about 15 minutes. You'll want to have your service menu ready (names, prices, and durations), a profile photo, and your bank account details handy for connecting your payment provider — OVIAH works with both Stripe and Square.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Step-by-step setup</h2>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">1</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Add your services</div>
<div className="step-desc">Go to <strong>Services & Pricing</strong> in your dashboard and click <strong>Add Service</strong>. Enter the name, price, duration, and category. You can also create add-on services that clients can bundle with a main service — like a deep conditioning treatment added to a haircut.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">2</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Set your availability</div>
<div className="step-desc">Under <strong>Availability & Hours</strong>, configure your working days, start and end times, slot duration, and buffer time between appointments. You can set a max number of bookings per day and choose whether clients book on a rolling window (e.g. 30 days out) or on a monthly release schedule.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">3</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Personalize your profile</div>
<div className="step-desc">Add your business name, tagline, bio, and profile photo under <strong>Business Settings</strong>. Use the page builder to set your brand colors, fonts, and section layout. Your booking page lives at <strong>[yourname].oviah.me</strong> and it's already live — make it yours.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">4</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Connect your payment provider</div>
<div className="step-desc">Head to <strong>Payments</strong> and connect either Stripe or Square through a secure OAuth flow. You'll use your own account — OVIAH doesn't hold your funds. Once connected, you can start collecting deposits and processing payments immediately.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">5</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Share your booking link</div>
<div className="step-desc">Drop your <strong>[yourname].oviah.me</strong> link in your Instagram bio, send it to clients, or add it to your linktree. You're live from the moment your account is created — clients can book 24/7.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Turn on deposit requirements before sharing your link. Requiring even a small deposit ($20–$25) dramatically reduces no-shows and sets a professional tone from the first booking. You can set a fixed amount or a percentage of the service price.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">What happens next</h2>
<p className="body-text">Once your profile is set up, clients can book directly from your page 24/7. You'll get an email and in-app notification for every new booking. Your calendar updates in real time, and appointment reminders go out to clients automatically based on your automation settings.</p>
<p className="body-text">If you run into any issues during setup, reach out to us at <strong>support@oviah.io</strong> — we respond within one business day (same-day for Studio Pro members).</p>
</div>
