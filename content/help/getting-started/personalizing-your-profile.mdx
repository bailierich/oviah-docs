<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Business information</h2>
<p className="body-text">Go to <strong>Business Settings</strong> in your dashboard to fill in the basics. These details appear on your booking page and in client-facing emails:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Business Name</div>
<div className="step-desc">The name clients see at the top of your booking page and in confirmation emails.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Tagline</div>
<div className="step-desc">A short line that appears below your business name — use it to describe what you do or set the vibe. Example: "Luxury braids & protective styles in Atlanta."</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Header Text</div>
<div className="step-desc">An optional headline displayed prominently on your booking page. Great for promotions or a welcome message.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Email & Phone</div>
<div className="step-desc">Your contact info for clients who prefer to reach out directly.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Social media links</h2>
<p className="body-text">Add your <strong>Instagram</strong>, <strong>Facebook</strong>, <strong>TikTok</strong>, and email so clients can find you across platforms. These appear as icons on your booking page and help build trust with new clients discovering you online.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Appearance & page builder</h2>
<p className="body-text">Your booking page is fully customizable through the page builder. Set your brand colors (primary, secondary, text), choose heading and body fonts, configure background styles, and arrange sections in whatever order works best for your business.</p>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH also supports dark mode — toggle it from the dashboard and the theme carries through to your admin experience.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Your booking page is live from day one at <strong>[yourname].oviah.me</strong>. Take 10 minutes to get your branding right before sharing the link — first impressions matter, especially when someone is deciding whether to trust you with their time and money.</div>
</div>
</div>
