<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Adding clients manually</h2>
<p className="body-text">From the <strong>Clients</strong> tab, click <strong>Add Client</strong> and fill in the details:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">1</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Enter their info</div>
<div className="step-desc">At minimum, you need a <strong>first name</strong> and <strong>email</strong>. Phone and notes are optional but useful — phone is required for SMS reminders.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">2</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Save the client</div>
<div className="step-desc">Once saved, the client appears in your list immediately. If a client with that email already exists, OVIAH won't create a duplicate — it links to the existing profile.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
<p className="body-text">Clients are also created automatically when someone books through your booking page for the first time. You don't need to add them in advance — OVIAH handles it.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Searching your client list</h2>
<p className="body-text">The Clients tab has a full-text search that checks first name, last name, email, and phone. Start typing and results filter in real time. You can also filter by client status to narrow down your view.</p>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Exporting clients</h2>
<p className="body-text">Need your client data outside OVIAH? You can export your entire client list as a <strong>JSON file</strong> from the Clients tab. This is useful for backups, migrating to another system, or doing analysis in a spreadsheet (most spreadsheet tools can import JSON).</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">If you're transitioning from another booking platform, add your most active clients first so they can start booking right away. You can always add the rest over time — or let them create themselves by booking through your link.</div>
</div>
</div>
