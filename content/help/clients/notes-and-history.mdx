<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Client notes</h2>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH gives you two types of notes on every client profile:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Notes</div>
<div className="step-desc">General notes visible in the client profile. Use these for preferences ("always wants extra foils on the left side") or context ("referred by Maya, first visit was a birthday gift").</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Internal notes</div>
<div className="step-desc">Private notes for your eyes only. Use these for sensitive info or reminders you don't want to accidentally share — like "tends to run 15 min late" or "prefers not to talk during service."</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Special notes and allergies</div>
<div className="step-desc">Dedicated fields for allergies and special requirements. These stand out in the profile so you never miss critical health or safety information.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Booking history</h2>
<p className="body-text">Every client profile includes their complete booking history — past and upcoming appointments with dates, services, add-ons, payment status, and notes. This is your record of the entire relationship.</p>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH also tracks lifetime stats automatically:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Total bookings and total spent</div>
<div className="step-desc">See at a glance how often someone books and how much they've invested. Useful for identifying your best clients.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">No-show and cancellation counts</div>
<div className="step-desc">Track reliability. A high no-show count might mean it's time for a conversation — or a block.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Last visit date</div>
<div className="step-desc">See when someone was last in. If a regular hasn't booked in a while, it might be time to reach out.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Take 30 seconds after each appointment to add a quick note to the client's profile. Over time, this builds a knowledge base that lets you deliver a personalized experience every visit — without relying on memory.</div>
</div>
</div>
