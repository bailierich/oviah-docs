<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">What's in a client profile</h2>
<p className="body-text">Every client who books with you gets a profile in OVIAH. The <strong>Clients</strong> tab in your dashboard is where you manage your entire client list — view details, track history, and add notes.</p>
<p className="body-text">Each profile contains:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Contact info</div>
<div className="step-desc"><strong>Name</strong>, <strong>email</strong>, <strong>phone</strong>. Email is required to create a client. Phone is optional but needed for SMS reminders.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Status</div>
<div className="step-desc">Active, Inactive, VIP, or Blocked. Status helps you segment your list and manage who can book.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Booking stats</div>
<div className="step-desc">Total bookings, total spent, lifetime value, no-show count, cancellation count, and last visit date. All calculated automatically.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Preferences</div>
<div className="step-desc">Communication preference (email, SMS, both, or none), preferred services, preferred time slots and days, and preferred stylist if you're a multi-provider business.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Personal details</h2>
<p className="body-text">OVIAH lets you store details that help you deliver a better experience:</p>
<div className="steps">

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Allergies and special notes</div>
<div className="step-desc">Critical for salons, spas, and wellness — record sensitivities so you never have to ask twice.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Birthday and anniversary</div>
<div className="step-desc">With birthday wishes enabled (Studio Pro), OVIAH sends an automated email on their birthday. Anniversaries are for your reference.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div className="step">
<div className="step-num">•</div>
<div>
<div className="step-title">Tags and priority</div>
<div className="step-desc">Add custom tags (e.g. "wedding party," "referral," "monthly regular") and set a priority level from 1–5 for internal use.</div>
</div>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<div className="content-section">
<h2 className="section-heading">Cycle-aware booking</h2>
<p className="body-text">For wellness and beauty professionals, OVIAH offers optional cycle-aware booking. If a client opts in, they can share their cycle data (last period start, average cycle length, period length) so you can recommend optimal appointment timing. This is fully opt-in for privacy and available as an add-on mod.</p>

<div className="tip-box">
<div className="tip-icon">Tip</div>
<div className="tip-text">Use the search bar in the Clients tab to find anyone quickly by name, email, or phone. You can also filter by status to see just your VIP clients or check who's been blocked.</div>
</div>
</div>
