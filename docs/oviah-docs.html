Getting Started

Setting up your OVIAH account

5 min read · Updated March 2026 · Written by the OVIAH Team
Overview

This guide walks you through everything you need to get your OVIAH account ready for your first client booking — from adding your services to going live with your booking link.

Before you begin

Getting set up takes about 15 minutes. You'll want to have your service menu ready (names, prices, and durations), a profile photo, and your bank account details handy for payment setup.

Step-by-step setup

1
Add your services
Go to Services in your dashboard and click Add Service. Enter the name, price, duration, and any add-ons. Clients will book directly from these when they visit your page.
2
Personalize your profile
Add your bio, profile photo, and brand colors under Profile Settings. Your booking page lives at [yourname].oviah.me and it's already live — make it yours.
3
Connect OVIAH Pay
Head to Payments and connect your Stripe account to start collecting deposits and processing payments. Takes about 5 minutes and you'll need your SSN or EIN for verification.
4
Share your booking link
Drop your link in your Instagram bio, send it to clients, or add it to your linktree. You're live from the moment your account is created.
Tip
Turn on deposit requirements before sharing your link. Requiring a deposit (even $20–$25) dramatically reduces no-shows and sets a professional tone from the first booking.

What happens next

Once your profile is set up, clients can book directly from your page 24/7. You'll get an email and in-app notification for every new booking. Your calendar updates in real time, and appointment reminders go out to clients automatically.

If you run into any issues during setup, reach out to us at support@oviah.io — we respond within one business day.
